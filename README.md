# Hi there, I'm Anand Singh 👋

I am a Digital VLSI Design engineer who believes that good hardware should be as cleanly architected as good software. 

Currently pursuing my M.Tech in Microelectronics and VLSI Design at IIEST Shibpur, my focus is on bridging the gap between theoretical processor architecture and physical RTL implementation. I specialize in writing robust, synthesis-friendly Verilog and building automated, self-checking verification environments.

### 🔭 What I'm currently working on
* **The Core:** Architecting the datapath and control logic for a 5-Stage Pipelined RV32I RISC-V Processor targeting FPGA implementation.
* **The Protocol:** Just wrapped up a Full-Duplex UART Transceiver utilizing robust 3-block Moore FSMs for glitch-free serialization. 
* **The Tools:** Deepening my expertise in Xilinx Vivado, behavioral simulation, and RTL synthesis.
